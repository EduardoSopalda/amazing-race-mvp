## Welcome briefing

> "Welcome, racers, to THE AMAZING RACE: BARCELONA!
>
> Today, four teams will race across Barcelona, solving clues, completing
> challenges, talking to strangers, getting lost, finding themselves
> again...and hopefully still liking their teammates by the end.
>
> This is not simply a running race. Speed helps, but navigation, creativity,
> intelligence and teamwork are what will win this.
>
> You may walk and use public transport where instructed. Do not run through
> busy streets, crossings or crowded pedestrian areas.
>
> Every challenge must be completed properly before you move on.
>
> Cheating, skipping challenges or deliberately interfering with another team
> may earn you a time penalty.
>
> Keep your phones charged. Keep your teammates together. And most
> importantly...
>
> DO NOT LOSE YOUR CLUES."

## Stop 1 - Arc de Triomf (41.3911, 2.1806)

Clue: "Barcelona welcomed the world here in 1888. Your adventure begins
beneath a red-brick gateway built for the occasion. Find it, and prepare to
go FOURWARD."

Challenge: Teams have 5 minutes to find random objects/poses and create
their own "Barcelona coat of arms." **Best photo gets a 3-minute head
start.**

## Stop 2 - The Mammoth, Parc de la Ciutadella (41.3880, 2.1860)

Clue: "A prehistoric giant is hiding nearby. He hasn't moved in over a
century, but today he's waiting for you. Find Barcelona's stone mammoth."

Challenge:
- Photograph the entire team apparently running away from the mammoth.
- Find and photograph: something representing Catalonia, another animal, a
  fountain or waterfall.
- Find a stranger willing to film a 15-second nature documentary about the
  team, beginning: "Here we see Team _____ in their natural habitat..."

## Stop 3 - Placa de Sant Jaume (41.3827, 2.1770)

Clue: "TWO GOVERNMENTS. ONE SQUARE. Leave the mammoth behind. Find the
historic Barcelona square where the government of Catalonia faces the
government of Barcelona. Once there, send the Race Director a photo of your
ENTIRE team in the square. Correct location = your next challenge."

## Stop 4 - Pont del Bisbe (41.3834, 2.1765) - "Lost in Barcelona"

Clue: "Find the bridge that looks centuries old...but isn't. Beneath it,
take a photograph with your entire team." Welcome to the Gothic Quarter.

Challenge - SIX photographs, every teammate in every photo:
- The famous bridge on Carrer del Bisbe.
- A gargoyle.
- Something Roman.
- A Catalan flag.
- A stranger willing to take a photo WITH the team.
- An incredibly narrow Gothic Quarter street.

Any order. **Bonus: first team to complete gets a 5-minute bonus, second
3 minutes, third 1 minute, fourth no bonus.** Do not necessarily tell teams
their position - tell them "Your photos have been approved. Proceed
immediately to your Roadblock."

## Roadblock - "Parles Catala?"

"WHO HAS THE GIFT OF THE GAB? Choose ONE teammate. Once chosen, you cannot
change. That racer must find someone who can teach them how to say in
Catalan: 'Hello, we're doing a race. Can you help us?' They must also learn
ONE additional Catalan expression of the stranger's choosing. Google
Translate is forbidden. Return to your teammates and teach THEM the new
expression. Send the Race Director a video of the entire team saying it.
Approval unlocks your next clue." When successful: "Molt be! Roadblock
complete."

## Stop 5 - Mercat de la Boqueria (41.3817, 2.1716)

Clue: "Enter Barcelona's most colourful market. Your mission is to find
something RED, something you have NEVER eaten, and something costing less
than 2 euros. Photograph all three together."

## Stop 6 - Carrer de Petritxol (41.3824, 2.1733) - Chocolate Challenge

Clue: "Leave the market behind and find a tiny street with a very big sweet
tooth. Chocolate has been tempting Barcelonans here for generations."

Challenge: Find and purchase one chocolate-related treat the team can share
(keep the receipt). One teammate closes their eyes. A second teammate may
ONLY use their voice to guide them. The blindfolded racer must feed a bite
of the treat to a THIRD teammate - no touching or physically guiding their
hands. Send the Race Director the video.

## Stop 7 - Temple of Augustus - BARCINO (41.3834, 2.1762)

Clue: "Long before Barcelona had this name, four columns stood above the
Roman city. Find them and discover what Barcelona was called then."

Challenge: Find the four ancient Roman columns. Answer: what was Roman
Barcelona called? Send a photo of the team at the columns plus the
one-word answer. **Wrong answers receive no response for 2 minutes.**
Answer: BARCINO.

## Detour - "Brains or Balls"

Choose one. Switching means starting the other from zero.

**BRAINS:** Answer five Barcelona riddles/trivia correctly. Example
questions given in the script:
1. What famous architect designed the Sagrada Familia?
2. What is Barcelona's famous tree-lined pedestrian boulevard?
3. What mountain overlooks Barcelona from the southwest?
4. What was Barcelona called during Roman times?
5. What language, besides Spanish, is traditionally spoken in Barcelona?

**BALLS:** Complete 30 consecutive controlled football touches as a team -
every member must touch the ball at least once; if it gets away, restart
the count. Send one continuous video of the successful attempt.

## Stop 8 - Botero's Cat, Rambla del Raval (41.3783, 2.1690)

Clue: "Cross Barcelona's most famous boulevard. Somewhere in Raval waits an
enormous animal. It doesn't roar. It doesn't bite. And despite its
size...it's a cat."

Challenge: The entire team must recreate the pose of the giant cat using
their bodies. The team cannot take the photo themselves - find a stranger
to take it. **Bonus: convince the photographer to also appear in a second
photo pretending to be a mouse, for a 2-minute bonus.**

## Secret Mission (Rambla del Raval area, approx. 41.3785, 2.1691)

Different per team:

- **Red:** Find a stranger willing to teach the entire team a dance move.
  Record everyone performing it together for at least 10 seconds.
- **Blue:** Convince THREE strangers to join the team and shout "VISCA
  BARCELONA!" simultaneously. Record it.
- **Green:** Find people from THREE different countries. Take one team
  photo with each; they must state their country on video.
- **Yellow:** Find a stranger and ask them to invent a completely new,
  ridiculous name for the team. Record them dramatically announcing it.

## Final Destination - Barcelo Raval / 360 Terrace (41.3790, 2.1692)

Clue: "You've crossed Barcelona. Now rise above it. Find the circular
rooftop overlooking El Raval. Your race ends at the top." DO NOT RUN INSIDE
THE HOTEL. Reaching the top does not mean you've won - one final challenge
awaits.

### Final Memory Challenge

Prepared cards/envelopes: ARC DE TRIOMF, MAMMOTH, SANT JAUME, PETRITXOL,
AUGUSTUS, BOTERO CAT. "Arrange these six locations in the exact order you
encountered them. You may NOT change your answer once checking begins."

Correct order: **Arc de Triomf -> Mammoth -> Sant Jaume -> Petritxol ->
Augustus -> Botero Cat.**

**If incorrect:** "That is NOT correct. Your team must wait 60 seconds
before attempting again."

**If correct**, closing speech (read aloud by the host, in placement order):

> "Team _____... After racing across Barcelona... After completing every
> challenge... After surviving the Gothic Quarter, Barcino and El Raval...
> YOU ARE THE WINNERS OF THE AMAZING RACE BARCELONA!"

Second place: "Team _____, you've crossed Barcelona and finished an
incredible race. You are officially Team Number Two!"

Third place: "Team _____, you fought all the way to the rooftop. You are
officially Team Number Three!"

Fourth place: "Team _____... You may be the final team to arrive... But you
escaped the mammoth, survived Barcino, conquered Raval and made it all the
way to the top. You have officially completed the Amazing Race Barcelona!"

Closing, once all four teams are together:

> "Four teams started beneath the Arc de Triomf. Since then, you've crossed
> parks, medieval streets, Roman Barcelona and El Raval. You've spoken to
> complete strangers. You've eaten chocolate blind. You've pretended to be
> cats. Some of you discovered previously unknown abilities. Some of you
> discovered that navigation is absolutely not one of those abilities. But
> all four teams made it to the top of Barcelona. So raise your glasses...
> To Barcelona. To completely unnecessary competition. And to our
> champions... TEAM _____! THE WINNERS OF THE AMAZING RACE BARCELONA! Salut!"

## Host emergency rules

- The host has final say. Safety beats competition, always.
- If somebody is struggling physically, allow their team to slow down, rest,
  or use appropriate transport. Don't announce why to the other teams.
- If a challenge location is unexpectedly closed or inaccessible,
  immediately mark that challenge as completed for all teams and send
  everyone onward.
- If a team's phone dies, allow another phone.
- If teams accidentally encounter each other, that's fine - teams may help
  one another.
- Teams may NOT sabotage, hide objects, give deliberately false directions,
  or interfere with another team's challenge.
- If a participant wants to stop, they stop. No debate.

**Suggested penalties:**

| Violation | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Minor rule violation | +2 minutes |
| Skipped requirement | +5 minutes |
| Deliberate cheating | +10 minutes |
| Dangerous behaviour | Host stops the team until it is safe to continue |
